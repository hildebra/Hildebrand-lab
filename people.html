The Hildebrand Lab.

We bring together researchers with complementary interests in microbial ecology, computational biology and bioinformatics. We value ambitious ideas, clear communication, collegiality and sustainable ways of working.

A team built around good questions.

Group leader

Prof Falk Hildebrand

Falk studies the ecology and evolution of microbial strains and develops computational methods for resolving them in metagenomic data.
