Join & collaborate

Bring your questions. Build what’s next.

We welcome conversations with prospective researchers, students, visiting scientists and collaborators. The best starting point is a clear note about the question you want to pursue and how it connects to microbial ecology, genomics or bioinformatics.

Opportunities

Ways to work with the lab.

Formal openings are advertised by the host institutes. For exploratory conversations, research placements or potential collaborations, email the group with a short introduction and relevant background.

01

Research roles

Postdoctoral and research positions are advertised through Quadram Institute Bioscience and Earlham Institute careers pages. Please use those listings for current roles and application processes.

Quadram careers ↗

02

PhD study

Potential doctoral projects may be available through institute programmes or partner universities. A good enquiry identifies the research theme, skills you bring and why you would like to develop them here.

Explore postgraduate research ↗

03

Masters & placements

We can discuss Masters by Research projects, internships and visiting research stays of three months or longer where there is a strong fit with ongoing work and supervision capacity.

Discuss a placement ↗

04

Collaborations

We are interested in collaborations that pair a well-defined biological question with thoughtful study design, useful data and an appetite for open, reproducible analysis.
