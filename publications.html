Publication archive · 2020–present

Lab-led publications, made clear.

A guided archive of recent papers, preprints, reviews and commentaries led by Hildebrand Lab researchers. Each card pairs the publication with an original conceptual figure and a plain-language summary.

At a glance

From microbial communities to the methods that resolve them.

This archive brings together recent work with a Hildebrand Lab first or senior author. Use the paper links for the full methods, data and figures; the visual notes below are designed to make the central idea easy to grasp.

2026 bioRxiv · Bioinformatics preprint

Protal: Ultra-fast metagenomic profiling and strain-resolved analysis ↗

Joachim Fritscher, Anthony Duncan & Falk Hildebrand

Introduces a fast alignment-based method that profiles microbial species and reconstructs strain relationships in the same workflow.

Under review Inflammatory bowel disease · Longitudinal study

Temporal IBD microbiome analysis uncovers ecosystem in distress

Rebecca Ansorge et al. · Senior author: Falk Hildebrand

Tracks microbiome change through time to reveal ecosystem-level signs of distress in inflammatory bowel disease.

2025 medRxiv · Preprint

Dietary bioactives increase gut microbiome diversity and alter host and microbial metabolite profiles ↗

Anthony Duncan, Federico Bernuzzi et al. · Falk Hildebrand & Maria H. Traka

Shows that a high-bioactive diet modestly increases microbial diversity while producing larger shifts in host and microbial metabolites.

2025 bioRxiv · Preprint

Optimizing storage, high-molecular weight DNA extraction and genome reconstructions from human faecal samples ↗

Klara Cerk, Anthony Duncan, Nabina Shrestha et al. · Falk Hildebrand

Compares storage and extraction workflows to preserve long DNA molecules and improve complete bacterial genome recovery.

2025 npj Parkinson's Disease · Clinical intervention

A prebiotic dietary pilot intervention restores faecal metabolites and may be neuroprotective in Parkinson's disease ↗

Janis Rebecca Bedarf, Stefano Romano, Silke Sophie Heinzmann et al. · Falk Hildebrand

Finds that a short prebiotic diet restores key metabolites and eases gastrointestinal symptoms, although microbial dysbiosis persists.

2025 Nucleic Acids Research · Methods

Detecting signatures underlying the composition of biological data ↗

Anthony Duncan, Wing Koon, Katarzyna Sidorczuk, Christopher Quince, Clémence Frioux & Falk Hildebrand

Introduces cvaNMF, a robust way to identify reproducible patterns in complex biological data, from gut microbiomes to cell populations.

2025 ISME Communications · Resources

KSGP 3.1: improved taxonomic annotation of Archaea communities ↗

Alastair Grant et al. · Senior author: Falk Hildebrand

Expands the KSGP resource to improve archaeal identification in amplicon datasets, bringing curated taxonomy and RNA-seq evidence into a practical workflow.

2024 BMC Genomics · Software

adhesiomeR: a tool for Escherichia coli adhesin classification and analysis ↗

Katarzyna Sidorczuk et al. · Corresponding author: Falk Hildebrand

Presents an open tool and database for classifying E. coli adhesins—the genes that help bacteria attach to surfaces and hosts—across thousands of genomes.

2024 Microbial Biotechnology · Review

Community-scale models of microbiomes: articulating metabolic modelling and metagenome sequencing ↗

Klara Cerk et al. · Senior author group: Falk Hildebrand & Clémence Frioux

Reviews how genome-resolved metagenomics and metabolic models can be combined to understand the functions and exchanges that shape a microbial community.

2023 Cell Host & Microbe · Ecology

Enterosignatures define common bacterial guilds in the human gut microbiome ↗

Clémence Frioux, Rebecca Ansorge, Ezgi Özkurt et al. · Falk Hildebrand

Reframes gut microbiome variation as mixtures of five recurring bacterial guilds, making gradual changes and disease-associated dysbiosis more interpretable.

2023 mSystems · Nutrition

A single dietary factor, daily consumption of a fermented beverage, can modulate the gut bacteria and fecal metabolites within the same ethnic community ↗

Sanjib Das, Ezgi Özkurt et al. · Falk Hildebrand & Mojibur R. Khan

Links consumption of traditional Apong beverages to distinct gut bacterial communities and faecal metabolite profiles.

2022 Microbiome · Software

LotuS2: an ultrafast and highly accurate tool for amplicon sequencing analysis ↗

Ezgi Özkurt, Joachim Fritscher, Nicola Soranzo et al. · Falk Hildebrand

Introduces a fast, flexible pipeline for amplicon data that improves reproducibility, accuracy and practical access to microbiome profiling.

2021 mSystems · Commentary

Ultra-resolution metagenomics: when enough is not enough ↗

Falk Hildebrand

Sets out a vision for resolving rare, within-population genetic variants in microbiomes—opening a route to observe evolution on ecologically meaningful timescales.

2021 Cell Host & Microbe · Evolution

Dispersal strategies shape persistence and evolution of human gut bacteria ↗

Falk Hildebrand, Toni I. Gossmann, Clémence Frioux, Ezgi Özkurt et al.

Shows that different gut bacteria persist through distinct strategies: some stay within an individual for years, while others rely on family transmission and dispersal.

2021 Microbiome · Methods

Much ado about nothing? Off-target amplification can lead to false-positive bacterial brain microbiome detection in healthy and Parkinson's disease individuals ↗

Janis R. Bedarf, Naiara Beraza, Hassan Khazneh et al. · Falk Hildebrand

Demonstrates how off-target amplification can create false bacterial signals in low-biomass brain samples.

2021 PeerJ · Genome resource

Extensive microbial diversity within the chicken gut microbiome revealed by metagenomics and culture ↗

Rachel Gilroy, Anuradha Ravi, Maria Getino et al. · Falk Hildebrand & Mark J. Pallen

Builds a large gene-and-genome catalogue that reveals hundreds of previously unknown chicken gut microbial species.

2021 Nature Aging · News & Views

Lifelong sex-dependent trajectories of the human gut microbiota ↗

Ezgi Özkurt & Falk Hildebrand

Explains emerging evidence that gut microbial communities follow sex-dependent trajectories across human life, linking host biology and microbial persistence.

2021 npj Parkinson's Disease · Meta-analysis

Meta-analysis of the Parkinson's disease gut microbiome suggests alterations linked to intestinal inflammation ↗

Stefano Romano, George M. Savva, Janis R. Bedarf et al. · Falk Hildebrand & Arjan Narbad

Identifies reproducible microbial shifts across ten cohorts that are consistent with lower short-chain fatty acid production and inflammation.

2021 Environmental Microbiology · Global ecology

Metagenomic assessment of the global diversity and distribution of bacteria and fungi ↗

Mohammad Bahram, Tarquin Netherway, Clémence Frioux et al. · Peer Bork & Falk Hildebrand

Compares 13,483 metagenomes across 25 habitats to map contrasting bacterial and fungal diversity patterns.

2020 Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal · Review

From bag-of-genes to bag-of-genomes: metabolic modelling of communities in the era of metagenome-assembled genomes ↗

Clémence Frioux, Dipali Singh, Tamás Korcsmáros & Falk Hildebrand

Explains why connecting functions back to individual genomes gives a clearer picture of what a microbial community can do—and how its members interact.

2020 Gut · Letter

Towards standardisation of naming novel prokaryotic taxa in the age of high-throughput microbiology ↗

Falk Hildebrand, Mark J. Pallen & Peer Bork

Proposes a systematic way to give stable, informative names to the many prokaryotic taxa discovered through sequencing.
