The world’s most diverse life is often invisible.

Our approach

Microbes shape every ecosystem. We develop the methods to resolve their diversity, track their evolution and reveal how communities change.

See our research approach →

What we do

Questions at the scale of communities, strains and genomes.

We combine computational methods with a patient- and ecosystem-centred perspective to turn complex sequencing data into useful biological insight.

01

Microbial diversity

Finding the organisms and genetic potential we have yet to catalogue, across human and environmental microbiomes.

  • Metagenome-assembled genomes
  • High-resolution strain delineation
  • Personalised microbiota care

02

Microbial evolution

Understanding how bacterial strains, genes and genomes adapt through time—and what that means for health and disease.

  • Population genetics
  • Genome plasticity
  • Comparative genomics

03

Microbial communities

Mapping ecological patterns in the gut, soil and water to connect community structure with environmental and clinical contexts.

  • Numerical ecology
  • Machine learning
  • Unsupervised clustering
